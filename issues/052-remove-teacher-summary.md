  rewrite-into-something-else.
- **Two maintainer rulings beyond the Reporter's flagging**, both *in*:
  1. **`EN_DAY` dies too** (`js/model.js:407-408` + export `:606`). Its only
     consumers are `nextDayPhrase` (`:558-559`) and `teacherSummary` (`:593`),
